What are the current maximum per diem meal allowances for client dinners in Tier-1 metropolitan markets (New York, London, Tokyo), and does alcohol require prior Managing Director written pre-approval?

Lexicon Policy Intelligence • Verified Codex v8.19 Synthesized in 420ms
Folio II: Corporate Travel & Hospitality / Precedent Clause 7.3.4
Statutory Certainty: High (99.8%)
Executive Summary

Client entertainment per diem is capped at $175.00 USD per person in Tier-1 metros. Alcohol expenditures are allowable up to 40% of total receipt value without pre-approval, provided an active corporate client executive is hosted.

Codified Operative Clauses

Clause 7.3.4 Geo-Matrix

Tier-1 Thresholds

Per-person dinner thresholds: New York ($175), London (£140), Tokyo (¥26,000). Tips and statutory gratuities up to 20% are reimbursable over and above this cap.

Clause 7.3.9 Hospitality

Beverage Protocols

Standard dinners do not necessitate prior written MD sign-off unless total table expenditure exceeds $1,200.00 or per-attendee total surpasses $250.00.

Clause 7.4.1 Substantiation

Receipt Integrity

Itemized merchant slips containing enumerated courses and attendees are mandatory within 14 business days. Credit card summary slips alone are voided.

Notice of Compliance Exception

Client engagements exceeding six (6) external guests require an advance informational email submission to the Corporate Finance Desk at least 48 hours prior to the reservation.
